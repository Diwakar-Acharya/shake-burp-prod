Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Shake & Burp, we collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Account Data: Name, email address, phone number, and encrypted password when you register.
  • Order Data: Billing address, shipping address, payment method metadata (we never store raw card numbers).
  • Customization Data: Uploaded artwork files (PNG, SVG), design coordinates, and canvas configurations.
  • Device Data: Browser type, IP address, and screen resolution for analytics and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders, including custom UV DTF printing.
  • Communicate order status, shipping updates, and transactional notifications.
  • Improve our platform performance, design tools, and user experience.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.

3. Payment Security

All payment processing is handled by Razorpay and Stripe, both PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors. We never store, process, or transmit raw credit card numbers on our servers. All payment tokens are encrypted end-to-end.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Custom artwork files are stored for 90 days after order fulfillment, then permanently deleted unless you request earlier removal.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting hello@shakeandburp.com. We will respond within 30 business days.

6. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, reach us at hello@shakeandburp.com or visit our Contact page.
